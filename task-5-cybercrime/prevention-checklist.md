Stay Safe Online: Prevention Checklist for Students 

1. Verify the URL: Always check the browser's address bar to ensure the website is official and uses "https://".
2. Hover Before Clicking: On a laptop like an MSI Crosshair 16HX, hover your mouse over links to see the true destination before clicking.
3. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Add an extra layer of security to your GitHub, LinkedIn, and email accounts.
4. UPI Safety (Financial): Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money. PINs are only required to send money.
5. UPI Safety (Verification): Always double-check the recipient's name in the payment app before confirming any transaction.
6. Avoid Public Wi-Fi: Do not log into your bank or student portal while connected to unsecured public networks.
7. Strong Passwords: Use a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols; avoid using your name or registration number in your password.
8. Report Incidents: Immediately report any fraud to the National Cyber Crime Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and the Helpline 1930.
