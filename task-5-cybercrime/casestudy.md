Phishing Case Study: The "Urgent Account Verification" Scam 


What it is: Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers masquerade as a trusted entity—like VIT Bhopal administration or a major bank—to steal sensitive data.


How it typically happens: > 1. The Hook: A student receives an email or SMS claiming their account will be deactivated within 2 hours unless they "verify" their credentials immediately.
2. The Deception: The message contains a link to a "login portal" that looks identical to the official college or bank website.
3. The Theft: When the student enters their registration number, password, or OTP, the attacker captures this data in real-time.
4. The Exploit: The attacker uses these credentials to access the student's actual account, change passwords, and steal personal or financial information.


Target: Usually college students who are often in a rush and may not check the sender's actual email address.


Consequences: This can lead to identity theft, financial loss from linked UPI accounts, and unauthorized access to academic records.
